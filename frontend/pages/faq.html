Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Explore common queries regarding accounts, security, and visual metrics below.

How does the interview tracker help my search?

By consolidating all applications, timelines, notes, and metrics into a single screen, you prevent missing round deadlines, record DSA question logs, and analyze where your pipeline bottlenecks are.

Is my resume and data secure?

Yes, all documents and records are securely stored with encryption profiles on our server databases. Your dashboard is private and only accessible with verified authorization tokens.

How are placement statistics calculated?

We compile your application logs (selections, rejections, pending offers) and run real-time aggregation queries to display visual clearance percentages and domain activity breakdowns on the analytics workspace.
