Cookie Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026. Discover how cookie session profiles are preserved on our platform.

1. Session Storage Tokens

We employ LocalStorage and browser session cookies to keep you authenticated when clicking between dashboard, applications board, profile workspace, and analytics pages. These tokens avoid requiring logins on every tab refresh.

2. Analytical Metrics Cookies

We use standard local session caches to store performance curves temporarily, reducing server loads and query response lag. We do not track browser histories outside of our platform domain.

3. Opt-out Choices

You can configure your browser tools to refuse cookies. Note that disabling cookies will disrupt system login statuses, preventing the dashboard from validating user credentials.
