Getting Started

Smart Interview Tracker is designed to keep your recruiting schedules clean and measurable. Follow these simple steps to set up your profile:

1

Create an account: Sign up using your student email address.

2

Upload active resume: Go to Profile Settings and upload your latest resume. This will automatically link to your applications.

3

Log application: Select Add Interview from the top workspace navigation to insert company details.

Pipeline Management

Updating Application Status

When a company invites you to an Online Assessment (OA) or technical round, edit the application card inside the applications board to change states. Keep question prep logs to refer back to prior rounds easily.

Visual Analytics Overview

The dashboard calculates ratios between total applied entries and offer selection outcomes, showing you difficulty ratings, selection trends, and packages.
